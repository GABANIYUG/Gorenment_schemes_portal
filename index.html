About the Portal

The Government Welfare Portal is designed to simplify the discovery of welfare schemes for every citizen.

We bridge the information gap between the government and citizens by providing a centralized, easy-to-navigate platform where users can find relevant schemes, check their eligibility, understand the required documentation, and seamlessly navigate to official application portals.
